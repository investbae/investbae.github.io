About

Connecting practice and policy

By combining hands-on experience in venture investment with academic expertise in capital-market regulation, we offer reliable analysis and policy alternatives.

Vision

A research partner grounded in data and law

We pursue honest research grounded in data and in law and institutions, so that participants in the venture-capital ecosystem can make sound judgments.

Mission

Evidence-based research for better institutions

Drawing on empirical analysis and comparative law, we diagnose the state of the venture-capital market and propose evidence-based improvements to policy and regulation.

Research Areas

Research & Advisory

We cover a broad range of law, regulation and policy surrounding venture capital and the capital markets.

Capital Markets & Venture Law

We study the policy implications and the legislative and reform direction of laws such as the Financial Investment Services and Capital Markets Act and the Venture Investment Promotion Act.

Digital Assets & Finance

We analyze regulatory trends and institutional design for digital assets, including investor protection for virtual assets and security token offerings (STO).

Venture Market Analysis

We diagnose the structure and flow of the venture-capital market — venture funds, the fund-of-funds, and policy finance — using empirical data.

Policy & Commissioned Research

We carry out commissioned research for public agencies and research institutions, providing the analysis and alternatives needed for policymaking.

Institutional Research Advisory

We provide academic and policy research advisory on regulation and institutions to organizations in finance and venture capital. We do not provide legal advice or representation in individual cases.

Comparative & Overseas Law

We comparatively analyze the venture-capital and capital-market regimes of major jurisdictions to draw implications for reform in Korea.

Important Notice

  • All research materials and analysis provided by the Institute are general information for academic and policy research, and do not constitute investment advice or a solicitation to invest in any specific security or financial product.
  • The Institute does not provide legal advice on individual matters; its materials are the result of general research on policy and institutions.
  • Research materials are based on the laws and data available at the time of writing and may change with subsequent amendments to the law or changes in the market.
