Research on venture capital and capital-market law and policy

The Korea Venture Market Institute (KVMI) is a private research institute studying law and policy in venture investment, capital markets and digital finance. We examine institutional questions using legal analysis and market data, and deliver research reports, briefings and discussion.

We do not provide legal representation, legal advice on individual matters, or investment advice.

Discuss a research project

Contact KVMI to discuss a commissioned research project or research-based policy advice for your institution — public agencies, financial institutions, venture associations and universities.

Research is led by Seung-Uk Bae. External researchers may participate in individual projects; they are not full-time employees of KVMI. Relevant outside roles of the lead researcher are disclosed to a prospective commissioning institution before a project is accepted, and a project is declined or its scope adjusted where such a role could impair the independence of the research.
