04 / OUR EXPERTISE

Law and data.
Two lenses. One inquiry.

Research & advisory↗
01

Capital Markets & Venture Law

We study the policy implications and the legislative and reform direction of laws such as the Financial Investment Services and Capital Markets Act and the Venture Investment Promotion Act.

02

Digital Assets & Finance

We analyze regulatory trends and institutional design for digital assets, including investor protection for virtual assets and security token offerings (STO).

03

Venture Market Analysis

We diagnose the structure and flow of the venture-capital market — venture funds, the fund-of-funds, and policy finance — using empirical data.

04

Policy & Commissioned Research

We carry out commissioned research for public agencies and research institutions, providing the analysis and alternatives needed for policymaking.

05

Institutional Research Advisory

We provide academic and policy research advisory on regulation and institutions to organizations in finance and venture capital. We do not provide legal advice or representation in individual cases.

06

Comparative & Overseas Law

We comparatively analyze the venture-capital and capital-market regimes of major jurisdictions to draw implications for reform in Korea.

06 / PEOPLE & PERSPECTIVE

The person behind the research.

About KVMI↗

The Institute’s research is conducted under the responsibility of a single principal researcher.

B

Seung-uk Bae Ph.D.

President & Director

A Ph.D. in commercial and capital-markets law, Dr. Bae researches the law and policy of venture capital, the capital markets, and digital finance. He conducted policy and institutional research at the Korea Capital Market Institute and Korea Venture Investment Corp., and now leads KVMI's research, education, and policy analysis.

Advisory & Committees

  • Advisor, Pension Investment Pool Alternative Investment Advisory Committee, Ministry of Planning and Budget (2025–)
  • Expert Member, Future Growth Potential Review Committee, IBK (2023–)

Current Positions

  • President & Director, Korea Venture Market Institute (2021–)
  • Adjunct Professor, Dongguk University (2023–)
  • Visiting Professor, Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (2022–)

Selected Experience

  • Research Fellow, Korea Venture Investment Corp. (KVIC), 2018–2021
  • Researcher, Korea Capital Market Institute (KCMI), 2015–2018
  • Secretary-General, Korea Economic Law Association · Korea Commercial Cases Association · Korea Trust Association
