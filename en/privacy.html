Privacy Policy

The Korea Venture Market Institute values your personal information and complies with applicable law.

The Korea Venture Market Institute (the "Institute") protects users' personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws, and processes personal information as set out below.

1. Items and method of collection

This form creates an email draft in your browser. This site does not send form entries to its server or save them in browser storage. Review and send the email yourself in your mail app. The Institute receives and stores sent emails through Hiworks (Gabia). This page cannot verify email delivery or server receipt. There is no membership login or telephone inquiry service.

· The name, affiliation, contact details (email/phone) and content of the inquiry that the user voluntarily provides at the time of inquiry.

2. Purpose of use

Collected information is used only to respond to inquiries and to discuss research and advisory collaboration, and for no other purpose.

3. Retention and use period

Collected personal information is retained for the period necessary to respond to the inquiry and to handle any related dispute, and is then destroyed. Inquiries received by email are destroyed within one year after their purpose has been achieved; where retention is required by applicable law, the information is kept for the relevant period.

4. Destruction procedure and method

Personal information whose retention period has elapsed or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay. Electronic files are deleted by means that make recovery impossible, and printed materials are shredded or incinerated.

5. Provision to third parties and outsourcing

The Institute does not provide users' personal information to third parties. However, in order to operate the website (hosting, access-log retention and security management) and to send, receive and store inquiry emails, the Institute uses the external services below, ensuring that they are managed safely and in accordance with applicable law within the scope of those tasks. Except where specifically provided by law, the Institute does not provide or outsource personal information to any external party for any other purpose.

Email: Hiworks (Gabia), for sending, receiving and storage of emails.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Security measures

The Institute limits access to personal information to the minimum necessary and takes the measures required by applicable law to ensure that personal information is not lost, stolen, leaked, forged, altered or damaged.

7. Users' rights

Users may request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of the processing of their personal information, and the Institute will act without delay in accordance with applicable law.

8. Privacy officer and remedies

· Privacy Officer: Seung-uk Bae (Director, Korea Venture Market Institute)
· Contact: office@kvmi.co.kr

For reports or consultation regarding infringement of personal information, you may contact the following Korean authorities.
· Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr, +82-1833-6972)
· Privacy Infringement Report Center, KISA (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118)
· Supreme Prosecutors' Office Cybercrime Division (1301) · National Police Agency Cyber Bureau (182)
