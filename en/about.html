01 — Overview

A private research institute for venture capital and capital-market policy

The Korea Venture Market Institute (KVMI) studies law and policy in venture investment, capital markets and digital finance.

We connect empirical analysis of venture capital and policy finance, research on capital-market and commercial law, regulation of digital assets and security tokens, and comparative study of foreign legislation and international standards. Research is conducted under the responsibility of a single lead researcher; co-researchers participate project by project and are not full-time employees of KVMI.

Seung-Uk Bae, Ph.D. in Law

Head and Lead Researcher, Korea Venture Market Institute

Seung-Uk Bae holds a Ph.D. in Law from the Graduate School of Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (February 2018).
