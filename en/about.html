About the Institute

A private research institute studying venture capital and the capital markets in depth.

The Korea Venture Market Institute (KVMI) is a private research institute studying the law, policy and institutions surrounding venture capital and the capital markets.

By combining hands-on experience in venture investment with academic expertise in capital-market regulation, we present analysis and alternatives grounded in primary sources, with data and methods disclosed, so that market participants and policymakers can make sound judgments. The Institute's research is conducted under the sole responsibility of a single principal researcher, and our work is prepared by giving priority, wherever possible, to primary sources and the text of the law.

Vision & Mission

What we stand for

Vision

A research partner grounded in data and law

We pursue honest research grounded in data and in law and institutions, so that participants in the venture-capital ecosystem can make sound judgments.

Mission

Evidence-based research for better institutions

Drawing on empirical analysis and comparative law, we diagnose the state of the venture-capital market and propose evidence-based improvements to policy and regulation.

Principal Researcher

Seung-uk Bae Ph.D.

President & Director

A Ph.D. in law (commercial law), Dr. Bae researches the law and policy of venture capital, the capital markets, and digital finance. He conducted policy and institutional research at the Korea Capital Market Institute and Korea Venture Investment Corp., and now leads KVMI's research, education, and policy analysis, alongside academic societies and university teaching.

Important Notice

  • The Institute conducts general academic, policy and institutional research and institutional research advisory. It does not provide legal advice, representation or legal opinions on individual matters, nor investment advice or solicitation regarding any specific security or financial product.
